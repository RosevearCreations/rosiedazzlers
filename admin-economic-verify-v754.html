ⓘ Configuration, proof conditions & safe outcomes

Where are public keys configured?

In the Rosie Dazzlers Cloudflare Pages project, under Settings → Variables and Secrets (or Builds → Build variables for the project's interface). Add the public base64-encoded 32-byte Ed25519 keys as ROSIE_ECONOMIC_OWNER_RECEIPT_PUBLIC_KEY_B64 and ROSIE_ECONOMIC_SOURCE_WITNESS_PUBLIC_KEY_B64, with separate identifiers in ROSIE_ECONOMIC_OWNER_RECEIPT_KEY_ID and ROSIE_ECONOMIC_SOURCE_WITNESS_KEY_ID. Redeploy after changing settings. Never put private keys in these variables, source code, or browser forms.

How exactly are signatures calculated?

Each issuer signs the UTF-8 bytes of rosie-economics-receipt-v754:<role>: followed by JSON of the documented ordered [[field,value],...] pairs. The role is owner or source. The owner payload includes SHA-256 of the complete canonical source transcript, not just a typed reference. See the Build 754 technical protocol and test-only fixtures in repository documentation.

What does a successful verification allow?

Only ELIGIBLE_FOR_MANUAL_CLOSURE_REVIEW. The owning original correction record must still be independently checked, with a separate authorized and attributable closure decision recorded in its source system. This build deliberately records source_correction_record_verified:false, closure_recorded:false and closure_allowed:false on every outcome.

What stays unchanged?

All prices, Basic Detail size tiers, travel rules, wages, consumables and stock, booking and accounting records, seasonal availability, communications and provider integrations. All missing or mismatched evidence remains HOLD.
