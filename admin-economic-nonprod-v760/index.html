ⓘ Help / safety

Which settings are required?

Server only: ROSIE_ECONOMIC_NONPROD_SUPABASE_URL, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_NONPROD_ENVIRONMENT_ID, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_ISOLATION_AUDIT_BASE_URL, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_ISOLATION_AUDITOR_PUBLIC_KEY_B64, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_ISOLATION_AUDITOR_KEY_ID, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_ROLLBACK_VERIFY_BASE_URL, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_ROLLBACK_WITNESS_PUBLIC_KEY_B64, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_ROLLBACK_WITNESS_KEY_ID, plus all seven previously pinned signatures. Two separate project hosts must have the .supabase.co form. No secrets belong in browser data.

What do signed approvals mean?

The isolated environment auditor signs project separation and safety-control findings. A distinct rollback witness signs an isolated restore result and a matched backup/migration/rollback hash. A subsequent owner signature binds those reports and the Build 759 DBA/security reviews to a single trial. No SQL bytes are accepted or executed here.

What is unchanged?

All live bookings, customer and staff records, published starting prices, payments, notifications, payroll, retention settings, append-only owner decisions and case closure remain untouched.
