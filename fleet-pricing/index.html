Fleet and workplace work is assessed around the actual vehicles, condition, location, access and requested timing. Rosie Dazzlers does not assume a vehicle-count threshold, discount, fixed recurring cadence or commercial rate before reviewing the request.

How pricing is confirmed

Scope first, then quote

Vehicle type, condition, service scope, travel/site access and coordination needs can materially change the work. A submitted assessment does not create a price or appointment.

What can affect a fleet quote?

  • Vehicle count, size, type and representative condition
  • Interior, exterior, full-detail or restoration-level needs
  • Same-location parking, safe work-area and property access
  • Salt, odour, pet hair, work-truck or heavy-soil requirements
  • One-time, seasonal, as-needed or repeat-service interest

What is not assumed?

  • No automatic fleet minimum or vehicle threshold
  • No automatic volume discount or commercial rate
  • No fixed recurring cadence or priority-booking promise
  • No contract or cancellation terms until explicitly agreed
  • No recurring billing authorization from an assessment form

Request the assessment first

Tell us who the vehicles belong to, approximately how many there are, where they are located, what condition or service concerns matter, and what timing would be useful. We will review that information before confirming a quote or scheduling path.

The fleet assessment is an inquiry only. It does not create a quote, booking, price commitment, discount, recurring-service agreement or recurring charge.
