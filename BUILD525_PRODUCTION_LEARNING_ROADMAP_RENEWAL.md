## Purpose
Reconcile the completed 516–524 cycle, close only concerns supported by dated attributable owning evidence, retain unresolved HOLDs truthfully, and renew the next bounded roadmap from observed Production outcomes without converting source/runtime GREEN into business or provider evidence.

## Evidence model
Continuing concerns remain classified as `retained`, `closed`, `owner_action`, `provider_dependent` or `unavailable`.

A concern is `closed` only when its owning authority supplies current attributable closure evidence for the same population, source identity, service/site, decision trace or observation window required by that concern. Exact-SHA source, Development or Production acceptance never upgrades missing provider, owner, recovery, authenticated-device, maintenance/fleet, experiment, remediation, seasonal, capacity, economics or manual-action evidence.

## Evidence-backed cycle learning
- Seasonal public-claim outcomes remain service-specific and owner-controlled. Publication, retain-HOLD and no-action states are accepted only when explicitly attributable; broad winter availability and source-owned temperature limits are never inferred.
- Winter booking/quote rule outcomes remain trace-bound and customer-transparency aware. Observed rule state does not authorize a new rule, and weather-ineligible sessions remain outside ordinary conversion interpretation.
- Controlled-environment routing remains service/site/workflow specific. A successful route or site confirmation does not establish universal indoor capability, future capacity or safe operation for another service.
- Provider/local-search closure remains source-identity, property/location, freshness and comparable-window bounded. First-party context is descriptive and cannot manufacture provider-owned closure.
- Recovery and authenticated-device evidence remain separate populations. Manual HOLD outcomes require current attributable observation and do not follow from source/runtime GREEN.
- Maintenance/fleet continuation remains bounded by explicit owner decision, participant/duration, capacity, invoicing, travel and stop-condition evidence; no enrollment or recurring billing is inferred.
- Booking/quote follow-up remains tied to the retained measurement lock, authorized comparable observations, weather eligibility and stop conditions. No winner, pricing, discount, booking or availability change is inferred.
- Staff/mobile closure outcomes remain materially like-for-like and confounder-aware. Closure does not prove causation or effectiveness; retain-open does not prove staff or device fault.
- Service economics, seasonal operability, observed capacity and technical reliability remain independent evidence domains. Missing comparable history stays insufficient, and provider billing/CPU/quota, recovery success, future capacity, price sensitivity and temperature thresholds are not inferred.

## Southern Ontario seasonal-service truth boundary
Rosie Dazzlers must remain useful through Southern Ontario cold snaps without pretending every detailing process is safe or practical at every temperature.

A service may be described as `cold-snap-capable`, `temperature-limited-outdoor` or `controlled-environment-required` only from explicit owning evidence. Exact working-temperature limits are carried only when the owning source records them. Technical application availability is not field operability, and a weather-limited service is not an application reliability failure.

No roadmap item authorizes a broad winter-availability claim, customer-facing temperature threshold, price change, automatic booking/availability mutation or seasonal promise inferred from weather, demand, margin, search movement, uptime or operator assumption.
