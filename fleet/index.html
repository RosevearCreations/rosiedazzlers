What helps us assess the work

A useful first request gives us enough operational detail to decide whether the work belongs in normal booking or needs a custom quote.

  • Approximate vehicle count, types and representative condition.
  • Town/service location, parking or work-area access, and relevant building or property rules.
  • Interior, exterior, full-detail, salt, odour, pet-hair, truck-box or work-use concerns.
  • Photos or shared media links for representative or worst-condition vehicles.
  • Your timing preference: one-time, seasonal, as-needed, repeat-service interest, or not sure yet.
An assessment request is not a quote, appointment, recurring-service agreement, pricing commitment, discount, minimum-vehicle promise, priority-booking promise or recurring-billing authorization.
