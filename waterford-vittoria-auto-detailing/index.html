Rural and seasonal contamination changes the labour

Waterford and Vittoria vehicles may combine normal town use with rural-road dust, work use, insects, winter salt and family or pet contamination. Routine surface soil can fit a maintenance package. Embedded salt, saturated carpet, heavy pet hair, persistent odor, paint defects or restoration work require a different process and often more time.

Photos are especially helpful when the contamination is concentrated or hidden below seats and mats. We stop and confirm a new scope before deeper work is added.

Prepare for the mobile visit

  • A stable, legal work location with enough room for doors and equipment.
  • Vehicle access and personal items removed from service areas.
  • Known damage, leaks, repairs, tint, coatings or fragile materials disclosed.
  • Customer-supplied water and power are not required for normal Rosie Dazzlers detailing.
