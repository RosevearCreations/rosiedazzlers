Choose the service by the vehicle’s actual condition

Tillsonburg vehicles can arrive with ordinary commuter dust, winter salt residue, pet hair, food spills, road film, or deeper interior and paint problems. Premium Wash is the lighter maintenance path; Interior, Exterior, Basic Detail and Complete Detail cover progressively larger scopes. Specialist work such as headlight restoration, salt treatment, paint correction, coating, odor treatment, heavy pet hair or deep carpet recovery is assessed separately when the condition demands it.

Photos are useful before booking when staining, oxidation, contamination or damage is difficult to describe. We would rather identify a restoration-level job early than promise a light-service price that cannot realistically cover it.

What we need at the appointment

  • A legal, reasonably level work location with safe room around the vehicle.
  • Access to the vehicle and removal of valuables or loose belongings that block the booked work.
  • Advance notice of known leaks, fragile trim, electrical issues, recent repairs, coatings, tint, or other conditions that may change the process.
  • No customer-supplied operating water or electrical outlet is required for normal Rosie Dazzlers mobile detailing.
