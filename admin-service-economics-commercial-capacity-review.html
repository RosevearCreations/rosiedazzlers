Service Economics Allocation & Margin Review Readiness

Margin review is bounded to service/package or add-on cohorts with explicit recorded revenue, material, labour, cash/refund and COGS linkage. Booking-level totals are never divided, weighted or spread by assumption.

Evidence and mutation boundary
  • Only aggregate economics, quote/pricing and fleet/commercial evidence is returned; customer names, staff identities and raw booking/quote identifiers are excluded.
  • Incomplete recorded material, labour, cash/refund or COGS evidence blocks service/package margin review.
  • Service/package margin requires a recorded booking-to-package linkage plus complete revenue, material, labour, cash/refund and COGS evidence for each included row.
  • Add-on margin requires explicit per-add-on allocation rows with recorded revenue, material, labour, cash/refund and posted COGS linkage. Aggregate flags or booking totals are not enough. Equal split, price-weighted split, percentage allocation and booking-cost division are not allowed.
  • Estimated overhead is not used to declare service/package or add-on margin readiness.
  • Quote-value or booking-stage patterns do not prove price sensitivity, discount need or price causation.
  • Inquiry volume and historical fleet work do not prove signed business or live scheduling capacity.
  • Live availability and collision revalidation remain authoritative at the existing booking endpoints.
  • No automatic price/discount change, add-on allocation, quote acceptance, invoice, booking, accounting/inventory posting, provider transaction, schema mutation or permanent polling is authorized.
