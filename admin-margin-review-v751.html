ⓘ What the figures mean

Recorded revenue comes from accounting. Consumable costs come from the original immutable posting rows (Build 750). Staff labour is an estimate based on recorded job time and hourly rates. Travel minutes come from Build 748 clock punches and are not payroll approval. A complete pre-travel contribution still does not prove full net margin because travel labour, overhead, tax and other costs may remain unallocated.

Why could a booking be “Review”?

Missing booking identity or mismatched vehicle size, incomplete COGS, stock-posting cost drift, unclosed travel punches, missing/unknown travel zone, unrecorded revenue or staff rate. These records must be reviewed at the owning workflow; this page will not fill in a zero or change an invoice.

What about winter services?

The seasonal bucket is based only on service date. Outdoor cold-weather restrictions, municipal requirements, controlled-site qualification and public-claim owner HOLDs remain governed by the independent seasonal workflows. This report never activates winter bookings.
