## SEO + Meta + CRM direction

Build 202 treats the source notes as a combined SEO + Meta + CRM strategy: SEO compounds over time, Meta can create short-term local demand, and CRM-style quote tracking turns conversations into measurable pipeline data.

## Useful takeaways we can use

1. **SEO remains the long-term engine.** Keep building local pages, one clear H1 per public page, strong title/meta wording, service-area proof, real before/after photos, and clear package copy.
2. **Meta ads are the short-to-mid-term lead engine.** Small local campaigns can create demand instead of waiting for people to search for detailing.
3. **CRM-style quote tracking matters.** Every lead should become a tracked quote so we can see open quote value, approved quote value, close rate, seasonal close-rate shifts, and package/add-on bundle patterns.
4. **Ad math should be visible.** Track ad spend, leads, booked jobs, cost per lead, customer acquisition cost, average ticket, and revenue/ad-spend ratio.
5. **Meta can support Google indirectly.** Some people see ads, then search the business name later, so branded search and direct Google form leads should be viewed alongside ad activity.

## Important caution

The examples in the source notes use large-market numbers and sample ad math. Rosie Dazzlers should start with small tests, local radius targeting, clear offers, and careful tracking before scaling any ad spend.
