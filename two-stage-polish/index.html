How the process usually works

  • 1. Condition review — photos help us identify the likely tier, but we keep the right to adjust after hands-on inspection.
  • 2. Preparation — Paint must be washed and decontaminated; a test spot determines the realistic correction target before the whole vehicle is attempted.
  • 3. Service work — Paint inspection, decontamination, test spot, defect-cutting stage and refining stage before protection.
  • 4. Re-check — we inspect the surface/material after the first treatment cycle before deciding whether more aggressive work is justified.
  • 5. Finish/protection — we complete the appropriate finishing, drying or protection stage instead of stopping at the visually dramatic middle step.
  • 6. Handoff — we explain the result, remaining limitations, aftercare and what would be required if a deeper restoration is still wanted.

What is not automatically included

  • unrelated repair work; deep scratches, thin paint, failing clear coat and edge damage set the safe correction limit
  • parts replacement, mechanical/electrical diagnosis, body repair or hazardous-material remediation unless explicitly quoted
  • extra correction/disassembly discovered after work begins unless we stop, explain it and receive approval

Before we arrive

  • Paint must be washed and decontaminated; a test spot determines the realistic correction target before the whole vehicle is attempted.
  • Remove valuables and loose items that block the work area.
  • Share photos and describe previous repairs/treatments or the cause of the problem when known.

Aftercare

  • Use gentle wash methods and suitable protection to reduce renewed marring; correction removes material and should not be repeated casually.
  • Keep the treated area clean and dry as instructed during any cure/dry period.
  • Contact us if the original problem quickly returns; recurrence can indicate an untreated source, damage or maintenance issue.

Frequently asked questions

How much does two-stage paint correction & polish cost?

The current starting price follows the condition table on this page. Final pricing depends on paint size, hardness, defect depth and correction target. Quote-required jobs are confirmed after condition review rather than charged as a blind flat fee.

Can photos give an exact quote?

Photos are very useful for triage and often establish a likely range, but they can miss depth, texture, hidden moisture, paint condition or access issues. We confirm the scope before doing work outside the quoted tier.

How long will it take?

Typical added time is 4–8+ hr. Severe conditions, drying/cure time or disassembly can extend the job.

Will it look brand new?

We aim for the best safe improvement, not an impossible guarantee. Deep scratches, thin paint, failing clear coat and edge damage set the safe correction limit. We explain visible limits before escalating the process.

Can this be added to any package?

Compatibility depends on the add-on and the preparation already included in the main package. Booking will show the available path; we may recommend a different base package when the add-on would otherwise duplicate work or lack required preparation.

Ready to confirm the right scope?

Choose the service in booking and include useful condition details or photos. Severe or unusual conditions are confirmed before extra work is authorized.
