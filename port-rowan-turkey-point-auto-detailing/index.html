Seasonal and lakeshore use can create concentrated problems

Port Rowan and Turkey Point vehicles can see summer travel, sand, insects and outdoor-use debris along with the same winter salt and everyday interior contamination found elsewhere in southern Ontario. A routine package may be enough for light buildup; deeply embedded carpet debris, odors, salt, pet hair, oxidized headlights or paint defects need a more focused service.

Photos help when a vehicle has been used seasonally, stored, or has one severe problem area. We confirm the likely tier and stop for approval if the hands-on condition requires deeper work.

What the customer provides

  • A legal, stable work area with room to work safely around the vehicle.
  • Vehicle access and loose belongings removed from the service areas.
  • Advance notice of known damage, leaks, repairs, coatings, tint or fragile materials.
  • Rosie Dazzlers supplies the normal operating water and power; customer utilities are not required for standard mobile service.

Pick the package for upkeep; pick the specialist page for the problem

Main packages cover routine interior/exterior combinations. Deep carpet recovery, salt treatment, pet hair, odors, headlight restoration, paint correction and protective finishes are separated because their labour, preparation and limits can vary substantially.
