Operating help

  1. Use interest statuses only to record staff follow-up: new, contacted, interested, closed, or unsubscribed.
  2. Scheduled and converted states require the booking workflow. Unsubscribed interest cannot be reactivated here; fresh customer consent requires its own approved path.
  3. Reminder candidates are calculated from completed-service history, opt-in state, vehicle identity and cadence rules.
  4. Use Reviewed for internal review, Contacted only after an actual staff contact outside this screen, and No contact needed when staff determines no outreach is needed now.
  5. Unresolved vehicle identity blocks Contacted and the UI does not offer that action. Resolve identity through the appropriate customer/vehicle workflow first.
  6. No action here sends email/SMS, reserves calendar capacity, creates a booking, changes reminder suppression, or authorizes recurring billing.
