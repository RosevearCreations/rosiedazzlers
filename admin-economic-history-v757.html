ⓘ Help and audit design

What is an independently signed history head?

A digest of a contiguous sequence of owning-system revisions, separately signed by a custodian whose public key was pinned by the owner. The signature and chain can detect alteration, but cannot prove that old database records are physically undeletable.

Can the owner audit write now?

No. The proposed append-only schema and RLS/revocation design require independent source DBA review, migration authorization, witnessed read-after-write validation and a separate owner closure decision. This preflight has no insert or closure endpoint.

How are custodian keys configured?

For server-only configuration, supply ROSIE_ECONOMIC_HISTORY_CUSTODIAN_PUBLIC_KEY_B64 and ROSIE_ECONOMIC_HISTORY_CUSTODIAN_KEY_ID, distinct from the owner and source-witness keys; never place private signing keys in a browser.
