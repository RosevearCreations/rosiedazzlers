Maintenance Plan operating help

Interest is not enrollment. The public form records demand and contact details only. There is no automatic enrollment, no appointment creation, and no recurring billing or charge.

  1. Review new interest in the waitlist above and confirm the customer’s preferred timing, vehicle mix and service area before making any promise.
  2. Use reminder candidates as retention prompts only. They are generated from completed-service history and reminder opt-in rules; they do not reserve calendar capacity.
  3. Any actual service still goes through the live booking flow, current availability, vehicle review, package/add-on scope, pricing and deposit rules.
  4. If the public waitlist is paused in growth settings, do not treat older interest as permission to enroll or bill a customer.
  5. A future paid membership or automatic recurring-service feature requires its own approved enrollment, cancellation, payment and customer-consent workflow before it can be enabled.
