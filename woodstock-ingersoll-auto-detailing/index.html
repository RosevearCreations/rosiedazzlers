Match the package to how the vehicle is really used

Daily commuting, family use, work travel and Ontario winter driving can produce very different detailing needs. A maintenance wash is not priced or processed like embedded salt, heavy pet hair, beverage contamination, oxidized headlights, paint defects or a vehicle that needs correction before protection. Our package and add-on pages separate those scopes so customers can start in the right place.

If the condition is difficult to judge, send photos. We can usually narrow the likely service tier before the appointment and will confirm deeper work before adding labour.

What we need at the appointment

  • A legal, reasonably level work area with enough room to work safely around the vehicle.
  • Vehicle access and removal of valuables or belongings that block the booked areas.
  • Advance notice of known damage, leaks, fragile trim, tint, coatings, repairs or electrical concerns.
  • No customer-supplied operating water or electrical outlet is required for normal mobile detailing.

When a specialist service makes more sense

Headlight restoration, paint correction, ceramic or graphene protection, deep carpet recovery, odor treatment, salt-stain work, pet-hair removal and other focused services have their own condition and preparation requirements. Use those pages when the problem—not just the overall vehicle—drives the job. For general upkeep, start with the package comparison and live booking planner.
