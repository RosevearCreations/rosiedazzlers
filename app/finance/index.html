Authority boundaries

What this cockpit can and cannot do

Can observe

Existing booking-finance events, provider-payment evidence, bank reconciliation, HST support, receivables, payables and checklist state through the retained read authority.

Cannot post

Journal entries, payables, remittances and other accounting postings stay in their existing explicit operator workflows.

Cannot charge or refund

Stripe, Square or any future provider mutation remains outside this read-only cockpit.

Cannot auto-close

“Close-ready candidate” means evidence has no computed blockers; it is not a closed accounting period and still requires operator approval.
