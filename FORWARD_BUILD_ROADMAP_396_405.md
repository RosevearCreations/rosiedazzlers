# Rosie Dazzlers — Forward Build Roadmap 396–405

**Planning boundary:** exact accepted source/deployment identities are resolved from live Git refs and exact-SHA workflow evidence rather than pinned into living prose.

## Product-direction rule

Every materially changed surface must remain useful on phone, tablet/small laptop and desktop. Public/customer, Detailer, Operations and Administration workflows must preserve readable layouts, accessible touch targets, truthful state, safe unsaved-work handling and server-authoritative evidence. A desktop-only path is not complete.

## Sequence

### Build 396 — Growth Baseline & Forward Roadmap Renewal
Establish privacy-respecting aggregate acquisition, conversion, booking, retention and commercial measurement from genuine observed evidence. Missing evidence stays unavailable; remain schema-neutral/read-only.

Retained Build 396 evidence discipline remains explicit: **Genuine observed evidence only**; missing data is `unavailable` or `insufficient`; **Anonymous acquisition/session evidence and exact customer-profile history remain separate layers**; and release acceptance requires the **exact Production SHA** rather than inferred deployment identity.

### Build 397 — Responsive UX & Growth Measurement Foundation
Create bounded Admin growth measurement and whole-platform responsive QoL authority. Correct material overflow, clipping, touch-target, navigation and responsive-table defects without persistent customer scoring or permanent polling.

### Build 398 — Customer Journey, Booking QoL & Acquisition Quality
Improve landing → booking clarity for vehicle size, packages, add-ons, inclusions, pricing context, unavailable dates, deposits and next steps. Preserve partial state where safely supported and keep attribution aggregate/truthful.

### Build 399 — Customer Communication, Self-Service & Booking Funnel
Improve confirmation, preparation, status, reschedule/cancellation guidance, completion/aftercare, review and rebooking while converging anonymous funnel evidence with server-authoritative booking status. Communications remain permission-aware and responsive.

### Build 400 — Detailer Mobile App QoL & Retention Evidence
Keep the canonical field workflow mobile-first with large touch targets, rapid evidence capture, checklist/material/add-on/completion shortcuts and non-authoritative local convenience aids. Repeat-service evidence remains exact-canonical-ID, aggregate, bounded and consent-aware.

### Build 401 — Job Handoff, Detailer/Admin Interaction & Commercial Evidence
Converge field-to-office handoff so Operations can understand genuine booking state, field findings, changed scope records, customer-action/review state, photos, recorded time, materials and closeout readiness without hunting through unrelated screens. Compose commercial outcome evidence only from existing authoritative booking/payment/job-cost/Finance facts. Missing fee, tax, material-cost, payment, balance or reconciliation evidence remains review/unavailable rather than estimated. No inferred busy time, payouts, hidden debt, forecasts or customer scoring.

### Build 402 — Admin & Operations Cockpit QoL + Growth Experiment Framework
Reduce administrative clicking/screen switching with a responsive Today/Operations cockpit for bookings, arrivals, active jobs, exceptions, incomplete evidence, deposit/payment issues, inventory shortages, callbacks/reschedules and close-ready jobs. Improve filters/navigation and define bounded approved experiment evidence without silently changing pricing, booking, consent or claims.

### Build 403 — Customer Retention, Rebooking, Service Guidance & SEO Growth
Use exact service history and approved evidence for explainable rebooking/service guidance while preserving consent and one meaningful H1, unique metadata/canonical/structured data, truthful Oxford/Norfolk claims and genuine proof.

Build 403 must also expand **every public add-on/service landing page with a professional, in-depth process breakdown** appropriate to that service. This includes, at minimum, staged **paint correction** assessment/decontamination/correction/refinement/protection guidance; professional **odor remediation** that distinguishes source removal, cleaning/extraction, deodorization and appropriate final treatment; and **water extraction / flooded-floor** restoration that explains extraction, trim/seat/carpet access where required, trapped-moisture inspection, drying, cleaning, mold/rust-risk mitigation and deodorization. The same depth principle applies to every other add-on landing page rather than only these examples.

Each add-on page must explain that scope, products, equipment, labor and duration depend on actual condition/severity. A light correction, minor odor or small spill may be comparatively simple, while heavy oxidation, embedded contamination, severe odor, saturated carpet/underlay or a flooded floor can require **several hours** of repair, extraction/draining, drying, cleaning and/or deodorizing. Pages must not promise a fixed repair time before inspection. Where severity materially changes scope or price, explain the assessment factors and customer approval path in plain professional language. Content must remain original, competitive, technically credible, locally relevant, phone-first and SEO-safe rather than copied from competitors.

### Build 404 — Error Recovery, Weak-Connection UX & Reliability Hardening
Harden customer/field/admin flows against duplicate submission, failed uploads, weak connectivity and opaque failures. Preserve drafts where appropriate, expose truthful save/upload state, bounded query/row-limit warnings and safe retry behavior without fabricating persistence.

### Build 405 — Full Responsive Production Acceptance & Roadmap Renewal
Run end-to-end Production acceptance across visitor → public page → booking → self-service → scheduling → Detailer field work → evidence/scope/customer approval → completion → payment/accounting → aftercare/review/rebooking, including aggregate growth/commercial/SEO/reliability evidence. Require representative phone/tablet/desktop acceptance and exact protected-main Production runtime proof, then renew the next roadmap from observed evidence/friction.
