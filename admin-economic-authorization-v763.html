ⓘ Help and safety

Who must sign?

Original thirteen distinct Build 762 signers remain mandatory. A new independent DBA authorizer, security authorizer, production operator and rollback-witness readiness signer must have four additional distinct public signing keys and four mutually separate HTTPS provider origins. The owner signs the tenth document with the already pinned owner key under a new production-decision domain.

Does “ready for external manual recheck” permit SQL?

No. It means the exact ticket, project, artifacts, operator readiness and rollback witness claims agree. An authorized human change manager must revalidate expiry, one-time use, the actual backup, the approved window, credential scope and two-person protocol outside this site.

What if any authority signs HOLD?

The hold is authenticated and surfaced. No operator/witness progression or incident closure is permitted; a separate signed decision and manual review would be required.
