How maintenance works

Maintenance is not a replacement for a first deep detail when the vehicle is already heavily soiled. Rosie brings standard detailing water and power; recurring service still needs a safe work area and any unusual parking, access, runoff, building, or property rules confirmed.

  • Start with a full or interior reset if the vehicle needs deeper cleanup.
  • Choose a repeat cadence after the first completed job.
  • Use photo links if the vehicle condition is uncertain.
  • Fleet or multi-vehicle plans should use the quote-first path.
