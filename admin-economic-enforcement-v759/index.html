ⓘ Configuration and safeguards

Which settings are needed?

Server only: ROSIE_ECONOMIC_STORAGE_AUDIT_BASE_URL, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_STORAGE_AUDITOR_PUBLIC_KEY_B64, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_STORAGE_AUDITOR_KEY_ID, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_DBA_REVIEW_PUBLIC_KEY_B64, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_DBA_REVIEW_KEY_ID, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_SECURITY_REVIEW_PUBLIC_KEY_B64, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_SECURITY_REVIEW_KEY_ID, ROSIE_ECONOMIC_NONPROD_SUPABASE_URL, and ROSIE_ECONOMIC_NONPROD_ENVIRONMENT_ID. All signing identities must be distinct from the four preceding custodians. Do not put secrets in JSON fields.

What exactly is audited?

An external fifth-key auditor's signed claim about nonproduction overwrite, delete, retention-bypass denial and prior-version recovery, bound to the precise historical source digest and provider version. Our site does not run those tests. The DBA and security chain signs the proposal for a future isolated trial, not permission to execute SQL.

Are any live business functions changed?

No. We do not change prices, bookings, consumables, payroll, payments, customer notices, winter operations or economic closure. Customer-facing starting prices stay unchanged.
