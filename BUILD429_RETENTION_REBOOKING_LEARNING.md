# Build 429 — Retention & Rebooking Learning

## Purpose

Use completed-job, rebooking, maintenance-interest and customer-communication evidence to understand what actually leads to repeat business without silently expanding outreach or consent.

## Evidence model

Build 429 may review bounded first-party evidence for:

- completed jobs followed by a later booking;
- rebook prompts and customer-initiated rebooking paths;
- maintenance-plan interest and eligible follow-up queues;
- customer communication delivery/acceptance evidence where available;
- elapsed time between completed service and later booking;
- service/package combinations associated with repeat bookings.

Counts are evidence, not causal proof. Missing identity-safe linkage, missing consent or missing provider delivery evidence must remain `partial`, `provider_dependent` or `unavailable`.

## Customer and consent boundary

No new marketing consent may be inferred from booking history, maintenance interest, account creation or prior messages. Any message dispatch remains governed by the current explicit-consent and communication authorities.

## Operator workflow

The preferred result is a read-only Retention & Rebooking Learning surface showing:

- repeat-booking evidence;
- maintenance-interest evidence;
- consent-safe follow-up readiness;
- stale or incomplete evidence;
- practical owner-review opportunities.

## Mutation boundary

No automatic email/SMS/push campaign, no consent mutation, no customer segmentation write, no booking creation, no maintenance-plan enrollment, no discount creation, no payment/provider action, no schema change unless separately approved, no accounting/inventory mutation and no permanent polling.

## Acceptance

The exact candidate must pass focused retention/rebooking authority, retained communication/consent and maintenance authorities, Current Source Gate, exact Development acceptance, protected-main PR checks and exact Production deployment/runtime/business acceptance.

Observed repeat behaviour may inform future decisions, but Build 429 does not claim causation from correlation.
