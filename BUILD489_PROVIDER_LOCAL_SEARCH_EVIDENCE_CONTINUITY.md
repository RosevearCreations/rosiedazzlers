# Build 489 — Provider & Local Search Evidence Continuity

## Purpose
Refresh provider-outcome and local-search continuity through one read-only operator view while preserving the retained owning authorities and their separate populations.

## Provider outcomes and communications
Stripe/PayPal payment outcomes, refund outcomes and definitive message-delivery evidence remain provider-owned. Continuity requires explicit provider evidence identity plus valid dated evidence in the retained provider trace. Source/runtime GREEN does not create a provider outcome and this release does not narrow the canonical HOLD automatically.

## Local-search continuity
Search Console and Google Business Profile continuity remains provider-owned and follows the retained Build 480 rules: same property/location identity, equal-length distinct dated windows and descriptive metric deltas only.

First-party referrals remain separate contextual evidence. They are not joined to Search Console/GBP provider metrics, customer identity or persisted bookings.

## Causation boundary
Seasonal messaging, Search Console/GBP movement and referral movement do not prove:
- ranking or indexing change;
- weather causation;
- winter demand;
- service availability;
- booking-conversion causation; or
- a service working-temperature threshold.

Southern Ontario cold-weather capability remains owned by the retained seasonal service evidence authorities.

## Mutation boundary
Read-only and manual refresh only. No provider contact, payment/refund/message mutation, Search Console/GBP write, booking/quote mutation, customer outreach, content/seasonal-message publication, schema/storage mutation or permanent polling is authorized.

## Acceptance
The exact candidate must pass the focused Build 489 checker/test, retained Build 480 local-search continuity, retained Build 476 provider traceability, Current Source Gate, exact feature-preview acceptance, exact-SHA Development deployment/runtime acceptance, protected-main governance and independent exact resulting-main Production deployment/runtime/business acceptance.
