## Why this scope is active

The maintenance retention surface can already display waitlist requests and vehicle-aware reminder candidates, but staff cannot operationally review those candidates or advance waitlist follow-up. The existing waitlist status-normalization code also does not match the live database status constraint. Historical vehicle-identity resolution remains a real separate gap, but durable booking-to-vehicle attachment would require new schema authority and is not being smuggled into this schema-free release.

## Active operating contract

- Staff may advance maintenance-interest rows only through `new`, `contacted`, `interested`, `closed`, or `unsubscribed`.
- `scheduled` and `converted` cannot be written from the retention workbench; those states require approved booking/conversion evidence.
- Reminder candidates remain generated by the existing vehicle-aware maintenance engine.
- Staff may record internal candidate outcomes only as `reviewed`, `contacted`, or `no_contact_needed`.
- Candidate review events are stored in existing `vehicle_history_events` with `customer_visible=false` and are derived server-side from the current candidate/booking/vehicle identity.
- Contact cannot be recorded for a candidate whose vehicle identity is unresolved.
- Recording a review does not send email/SMS, suppress the reminder engine, create a booking, reserve capacity, mark conversion, change payment state, or enable recurring billing.
- The staff surface is `/admin-maintenance-retention.html`.
- The live database currently has no completed bookings, so executable synthetic tests are the current reminder-candidate proof floor.
