ⓘ Help and safeguards

Does acknowledged handoff mean the owner approved renewal?

No. The source signs an acknowledgment claim. Only the real owner can separately execute a business renewal.

When is dispute aging considered current?

The independent source signs elapsed hours, open disputes, exact original disposition register and source-derived SLA threshold. Any open, conflicting or overdue claim remains HOLD.

Can a retention witness close a case?

No. It is bounded readiness evidence with a signed expiry. The real original custodian and owner must separately authorize any closure. This page never writes to their databases.
