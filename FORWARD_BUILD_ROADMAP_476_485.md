## Southern Ontario seasonal-service truth direction
Rosie Dazzlers must remain useful through Southern Ontario cold snaps without pretending every detailing process is safe or practical at every temperature.

For Builds 480–485:
- search/referral changes never prove winter service availability or weather causation;
- exact minimum/maximum working temperatures must come from explicit product, equipment, water/ice, site or service-process evidence rather than guessed thresholds;
- service availability should eventually distinguish cold-snap-capable work, temperature-limited outdoor work and work requiring a garage/controlled environment;
- booking/quote experiments must not count weather-ineligible sessions as ordinary conversion failures;
- staff/mobile evidence must not blame staff or devices for weather/site restrictions; and
- Build 485 must renew the roadmap with a dedicated cold-weather capability/transparency release before broad winter claims are published.
