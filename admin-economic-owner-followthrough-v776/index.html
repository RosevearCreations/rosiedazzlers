ⓘ Help and safety

Does an authenticated follow-through claim complete the original closure?

No. An original owner and authorized custodian must separately decide and execute any real closure.

What is an aging exception?

The independently signed current case age, original alert threshold and open exception count must agree with the predecessor record. Overdue, missing or conflicting evidence remains HOLD.

Does custody renewal modify stored source records?

No. A signed custody renewal claim has a bounded expiry and links an unchanged original retained-source digest. Real database or storage controls remain outside this review.

What happens when Build 778 finds an expired or conflicting receipt?

It keeps the case on HOLD and identifies the independent issuer, custodian and owner follow-up needed. It cannot extend an expiry or create an approval on their behalf.

Does the Build 779 register approve or save custody decisions?

No. It displays three source-bound external decision requirements and offers an unsigned local download only. Actual original-owner authorization, custodian renewal, external dispute closure and audit retention stay with their independent issuers.
