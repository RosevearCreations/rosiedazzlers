Before your appointment

Use this checklist before booking or before we arrive.

  • Remove personal items, car seats if needed, valuables, and loose garbage.
  • Confirm driveway or safe work area access.
  • Tell us if water or power is not available.
  • Send photos or share links for stains, pet hair, salt, odour, paint, or headlights.
  • Let us know about weather, locked gates, pets, or access limits.

Ready for the next step?

Choose a time online, send photo links for a quote-first review, or contact us if the vehicle needs special handling.
