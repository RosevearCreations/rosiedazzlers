What Paint Correction Actually Does

Paint correction can improve gloss, oxidation, haze, and many surface-level swirl marks. It cannot replace missing clear coat, fix deep gouges, or repair body damage.

Honest result language

Correction should be quoted carefully because paint condition, clear coat, damage, and customer expectations all matter.

  • Photos help identify swirl marks, oxidation, scratches, and coating-prep needs.
  • A one-step polish is different from a two-step correction.
  • Ceramic coating should lock in a properly prepared finish, not hide poor paint prep.
