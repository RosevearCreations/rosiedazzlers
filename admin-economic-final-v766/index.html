ⓘ Help and safety boundaries

What does explicit owner authorization permit?

Only a separately controlled external manual closure review identified by a new authorization ticket. The signed owner decision may instead be HOLD. There is no executable approval, replay ledger or closure endpoint here.

How is the append-only decision receipt reviewed?

A separately pinned custodian signer confirms an externally recorded decision hash, previous ledger hash, retention and storage-enforcement proof. Source storage must still be independently audited; a website signature check is not WORM proof.

Can all GREEN claims close the booking automatically?
