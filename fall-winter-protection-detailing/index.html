Best starting point

Exterior Detail is the normal starting recommendation. Quick Book keeps the current vehicle-size price, availability, deposit and add-on rules authoritative.

Prepare first, protect second

1. Clean the surface

Remove normal road film from paint, wheels, glass and exterior trim before judging what the vehicle actually needs.

2. Review contamination and defects

Bonded contamination, tar, rough paint, swirls, oxidation or neglected trim can change the prep required before a protection product is useful.

3. Match protection to the goal

A maintenance-friendly sealant or ceramic-spray path is different from a coating project that needs deeper preparation. Rosie keeps those scopes separate.

Protection options already in the Rosie catalogue

  • Full clay treatment when bonded contamination needs removal before protection.
  • High-grade paint sealant for a dedicated protection step.
  • Ceramic spray wax for a maintenance-oriented protection option.
  • Windshield ceramic coating where glass protection is the goal.
  • Trim restoration when faded exterior trim needs attention.
  • Ceramic coating only when the prep, condition and longer-term goal support that larger project.

When we pause and re-quote

  • paint contamination needs more decontamination than the starting service assumes;
  • swirls, oxidation or scratches lead to a separate correction discussion;
  • the customer moves from a maintenance protection product into a coating project;
  • weather, site conditions or cure requirements make the requested protection unsuitable for that appointment.

What this page does not include

This is detailing-surface protection for paint, glass and trim. It is not mechanical rustproofing, oil spraying, undercoating or underbody corrosion treatment. Those are different services with different equipment, access and insurance/environmental considerations.

When photos are useful

Send exterior photos if the paint is rough, dull, swirled, scratched, oxidized, heavily tarred or previously coated. Photos help Rosie distinguish a straightforward exterior/protection visit from a correction or coating-prep project before you commit to the wrong scope.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the most expensive protection option before winter?

No. The useful choice depends on vehicle condition, how long you want the protection to last, maintenance expectations and whether the surface needs correction first.

Can you apply protection without clay or polishing?

Sometimes. Rosie uses the least work needed for the condition and goal. Clay or correction should be added only when the surface actually needs that preparation.

Can I combine winter prep with interior salt prevention or cleanup?

Yes. You can review the main package and add-ons in Quick Book, or use the Spring Salt Recovery path later when winter contamination has accumulated.
