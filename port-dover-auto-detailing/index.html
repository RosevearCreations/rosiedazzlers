Seasonal driving can change what the vehicle needs

Port Dover vehicles may see ordinary commuting mixed with shoreline wind, sand, insects, summer travel and Ontario winter contamination. Light road film can fit a maintenance service; embedded salt, heavy interior debris, odors, pet hair, stained carpet, oxidized headlights or damaged paint need deeper condition-based work.

If one problem dominates the job, use its specialist page or send photos before booking. We confirm realistic limits and any expanded labour before moving beyond the accepted scope.

What the mobile appointment requires

  • A legal, reasonably level work area with safe space around the vehicle.
  • Vehicle access and loose belongings removed from the booked work areas.
  • Advance notice of known damage, leaks, fragile trim, tint, coatings or recent repairs.
  • No customer-supplied operating water or electrical outlet is required for normal service.

Choose maintenance or restoration deliberately

Premium Wash and the main Interior, Exterior, Basic and Complete packages cover routine scopes. Salt treatment, carpet recovery, odor removal, headlight restoration, paint correction and protective finishes are separate because condition, preparation and time can vary widely. The service pages explain those decision points before checkout.
