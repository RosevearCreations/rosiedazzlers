Condition matters more than the postal code

Vehicles used across Zorra, Thamesford and Embro can range from routine family commuters to rural and work vehicles carrying dust, soil, salt and heavier interior contamination. The same vehicle size can therefore represent very different labour. We use package scope plus condition to decide whether the job is maintenance, deep cleaning or specialist restoration.

Send photos for severe salt, spills, heavy pet hair, paint defects, oxidized lights, unusual contamination or anything likely to need repeated treatment.

Site and vehicle readiness

  • A legal, stable and reasonably level work area with safe clearance.
  • Vehicle access and loose cargo removed from booked areas.
  • Known mechanical/electrical concerns, fragile parts and previous treatments disclosed.
  • Normal Rosie Dazzlers service does not require a customer hose or electrical outlet.

Maintenance packages and specialist work stay separate

Routine package cleaning is the right path for normal upkeep. Deep carpet recovery, salt treatment, pet hair, odors, engine-bay cleaning, headlight restoration, paint correction and coating/protection are separate because the labour and risk can change quickly with condition.
