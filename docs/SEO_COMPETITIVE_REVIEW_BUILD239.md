# Build 239 SEO and competitive sanity review

**Updated:** August 1, 2026

## Current Google direction

- Title links should use concise, descriptive, distinct titles and avoid keyword stuffing or repetitive boilerplate.
- The page's main visual title should be clear and distinctive; Rosie continues to enforce no more than one H1 per HTML page.
- Google Business Profile local results are primarily affected by relevance, distance, and prominence. There is no legitimate first-page guarantee.
- Complete profile information, accurate categories/service areas/hours, approved photos, legitimate reviews, and helpful review responses support customer trust and local visibility.

Sources:
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en-CA
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/7249669?hl=en

## Current detailing-site observations

Current Ontario mobile-detailing sites commonly emphasize a clear service area, recognizable service names, authentic before/after proof, owner/operator trust, simple booking/contact actions, and concise local headings. Rosie already has deeper booking and operational capability; the strongest public-facing gap remains approved Rosie-owned proof and a consistent review/photo cadence rather than more thin or duplicated landing pages.

Reference examples reviewed:
- https://steadfastdetailing.ca/
- https://www.washmenow.ca/
- https://lvscardetail.com/
