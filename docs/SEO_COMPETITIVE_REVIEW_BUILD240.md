# SEO and Competitive Review — Build 240

**Updated:** 2026-08-05

## Current direction

Rosie Dazzlers should keep one clear H1, concise descriptive titles, distinct service and town-page intent, accurate Oxford County/Norfolk County wording, useful internal links, direct booking actions, visible package/service information, and customer-approved local photographic proof. No build can guarantee first-page placement because local results also depend on distance, prominence, competition, reviews, profile completeness, and search context.

## Current competitor patterns reviewed

Regional detailing sites continue to emphasize mobile convenience, recognizable service names, package/pricing context, ceramic coating and paint correction, before/after proof, service areas, and direct call/booking actions. Rosie Dazzlers already has stronger operational tooling; the next public-facing advantage should be authentic local proof, completed Google Business Profile information, review cadence, and dependable mobile booking rather than duplicating pages or stuffing place names.

## Build 240 SEO safeguards

- One-H1 release validation remains mandatory.
- Protected admin pages remain noindex.
- Existing public title/description length checks remain active.
- The new inventory page is internal and does not create duplicate public search intent.
- Product publishing should remain blocked until image, price, tax, stock, category, description, and SEO requirements are complete.
- Replace high-value placeholders with consented Rosie-owned photos before broad promotion.

## Primary guidance references

- Google Search Central: title links, sitelinks/internal linking, site names, and LocalBusiness structured data.
- Google Business Profile: relevance, distance, prominence, accurate business representation, reviews, photos, hours, services, and service area.
