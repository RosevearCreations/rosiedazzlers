# SEO and Competitive Review — Build 238

**Date:** 2026-07-30

## Direction retained

- One clear H1 per exposed page.
- Concise, descriptive titles and descriptions aligned with actual page intent.
- Distinct service pages for paint correction, ceramic coating, interior work, odour/pet-hair and other real offerings.
- Distinct local/service-area pages only where content is genuinely useful and locally specific.
- Accurate business name, phone, service area, hours, services and approved photos shared consistently with Google Business Profile.
- Internal links that help customers move from problem/service information to pricing, proof and booking.
- Authentic Rosie-owned before/after proof; no invented reviews, locations, claims or rankings.

## Build 238 changes

Nineteen long titles or descriptions were shortened while preserving mobile auto detailing, Oxford County/Norfolk County, relevant service terms, pricing/booking intent and readable wording. No keyword-stuffed duplicate pages or ranking guarantees were added.

## Competitive observations

Current local detailing sites commonly make packages/pricing, specialty services, before/after proof, local wording, reviews and booking/contact actions prominent. Rosie Dazzlers should compete through clearer operational trust, proof-of-work, transparent policies, direct booking and rich customer follow-through rather than copying competitor wording.
