## Current Google direction retained

- Keep each indexable page focused around one descriptive, concise title and one clear main H1. Do not multiply near-identical headings or stuff town/service variants into the title.
- Local visibility is not guaranteed by code or keyword density. Google describes local results as primarily influenced by relevance, distance, and prominence. Keep the Business Profile complete and accurate, maintain hours/categories/service information, earn genuine reviews, respond to reviews, and add useful real photos/video.
- Keep local structured data factual. A town landing page should describe the town/county it actually serves rather than claiming every service town on every local page.
- Prefer authentic Rosie-owned before/after and process proof as it becomes consent-approved. The DAIP raw-media system remains private; only reviewed derivatives may later become public proof.
