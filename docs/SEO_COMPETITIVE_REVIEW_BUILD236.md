# SEO and Competitive Direction Review — Build 236

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-26

## Direction retained

Rosie Dazzlers should compete through clear local relevance and conversion usefulness rather than copying another detailer's wording. Keep one descriptive H1, a distinct title and description, visible town/service context, direct booking and photo-estimate actions, helpful service explanations, honest pricing context, authentic before/after proof with consent, internal links to relevant FAQs/help, and fast responsive media.

## Current implementation priorities

1. LocalBusiness structured data on the business/location context actually visible on the page.
2. Product structured data only for genuine sellable products with matching visible price/availability content.
3. Descriptive image filenames, alt text, captions, stable dimensions, responsive sources, and approved local proof.
4. Core Web Vitals work driven by measured pages, especially hero/gallery images, layout shifts, and unnecessary JavaScript.
5. People-first service copy explaining process, suitability, limitations, preparation, and aftercare.
6. Town pages that remain useful and distinct rather than thin location-name swaps.
7. Search Console and Google Business Profile evidence before making broad title/location changes.

## Competitor presentation observations

Current detailer sites continue to make service descriptions, pricing/context, booking calls-to-action, mobile contact paths, and specialty-service pages prominent. Rosie Dazzlers already has the stronger operational foundation; the near-term gap is approved local visual proof and verified end-to-end conversion reliability, not more duplicate service pages.

## Guardrails

- No guaranteed rankings.
- No fabricated reviews, service-area claims, addresses, results, or photographs.
- No schema content that is absent from the visible page.
- No automatic publication of unapproved customer media.
- No multiple competing H1 headings on public pages.
