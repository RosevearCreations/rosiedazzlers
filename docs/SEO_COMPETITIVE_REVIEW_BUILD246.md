# SEO and Competitive Review — Build 246

## Direction retained

- one prominent H1 per exposed page
- concise, descriptive and distinct title elements
- location wording that reflects real service areas rather than invented offices
- complete, accurate Google Business Profile information
- descriptive image filenames and alt text
- authentic reviews and approved local before/after proof
- prominent booking actions and clear mobile-service explanations

## Build 246 relevance

Catalog readiness helps prevent thin or confusing public records from being indexed. It does not guarantee ranking. Public items should have meaningful names, categories, real images and enough useful information for a customer to understand what is offered.

## Competitive observation

Current regional mobile-detailing sites continue to emphasize that service comes to the customer, recognizable service categories, flexible scheduling and direct online booking. Rosie Dazzlers should retain those simple public messages while using its deeper operational system behind the scenes.
