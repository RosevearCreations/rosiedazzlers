# Build 245 SEO and Competitive Review

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-06

## Search direction retained

- Keep a concise descriptive title and one clearly prominent main heading per public page.
- Keep service and location pages distinct rather than creating near-duplicate town pages.
- Use descriptive image filenames and alt text.
- Maintain accurate service areas, services, hours, phone details, photos and legitimate reviews in Google Business Profile.
- Do not promise first-page placement; local visibility depends on relevance, distance and prominence/popularity signals.

## Competitive observations

Current mobile detailing sites continue to make mobile convenience, recognizable service names, pricing context, ceramic coating, direct booking and trust proof prominent. Rosie Dazzlers already has deeper booking and operations tooling. Its highest-value public differentiators remain clear mobile-service coverage, transparent package context, approved local before/after proof, easy booking and dependable follow-up.
