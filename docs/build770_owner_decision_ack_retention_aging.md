# Rosie Dazzlers Build 770 — Independent Owner Acceptance Decision Register, External Handoff Acknowledgment & Retention Exception Aging

**Read-only original-source claim review.** No production SQL, audit append, immutable storage implementation, business acceptance, external case reopening, customer notification, or actual original record mutation is performed by this application. Authentic original signers, external providers and genuine historical receipts are **not bundled**; missing evidence remains HOLD regardless of a GREEN software release.

## Independent original issuer chain

1. Signed-in `manage_staff` owner review collects four exact case identifiers and the four original owner-signed JSON envelopes from Builds 764–768. Every review re-fetches the complete external operator, inspector, DBA/audit, custody, reopening, handoff and retention chain through Build 769; client summaries are never trusted.
2. A distinct independent **owner decision-register witness** serves `rosie-independent-owner-decision-register-v770`, signed over `REGISTER_FIELDS770` with domain `rosie-independent-owner-decision-register-v770:`. Its previous and current decision-register SHA-256 values must differ; the new entry binds original owner decision, externally witnessed handoff, reopening outcome and retention receipts. The three permitted outcomes are `OWNER_MANUAL_ACCEPTANCE_REVIEW_REGISTERED_CLAIM` (only with a clean earlier manual-review chain), `OWNER_EXTERNAL_REOPEN_FOLLOWUP_REGISTERED_CLAIM` (only with an original signed reopen request and external reopening claim), and `OWNER_HOLD_REGISTERED_CLAIM`. All are externally signed *claims*, not a website owner acceptance or case update.
3. A different independent **handoff acknowledgment witness** serves `rosie-independent-owner-handoff-acknowledgment-v770`, signed over `ACK_FIELDS770` with domain `rosie-independent-owner-handoff-acknowledgment-v770:`. A clean `OWNER_EVIDENCE_RECEIPT_ACKNOWLEDGED_CLAIM` requires the exact original handoff manifest and authenticated decision-register receipt. Disputed or stale acknowledgment remains HOLD.
4. A third **retention exception-aging witness** serves `rosie-retention-exception-aging-v770`, signed over `AGING_FIELDS770` with domain `rosie-retention-exception-aging-v770:`. The snapshot binds prior authenticated decision and acknowledgment, external retained-source manifest, retention policy digest and retained-through time. Clean `NO_OPEN_RETENTION_EXCEPTION_CLAIM` requires zero open and critical exceptions and clean earlier retention. A signed `OPEN_RETENTION_EXCEPTIONS_CLAIM` or `RETENTION_REGISTER_DISPUTED_HOLD` never silently clears exceptions. The oldest open exception's age is calculated from original issuer-signed timestamps; its externally signed `alert_after_hours` (1–4320) is an **advisory review threshold, not a statutory retention period**. Critical exceptions, aged exceptions, disputes and expired retention windows remain HOLD.
5. The owner sees separate statuses, actionable exception follow-ups, signed aging measurements and an explicitly **unsigned** JSON review export. Real owner business acceptance, external case reopening and independent durable storage inspection must be confirmed separately by their original authorities.

## Trust, configuration, and safety

- All **33** signing public keys/IDs must be distinct (27 original roles, 3 Build 769 roles, 3 Build 770 roles). All **24** server-controlled original issuer HTTPS base URLs must also be distinct. Canonical signed field sets and role-specific domains reject extra fields, cross-role replay and mismatched hashes. Claims must be chronologically ordered, no older than seven days and at most five minutes in the future.
- New issuer configurations (each with `PUBLIC_KEY_B64`, `KEY_ID`, `BASE_URL`) are prefixed `ROSIE_ECONOMIC_ORIGINAL_OWNER_DECISION_REGISTER_WITNESS_`, `ROSIE_ECONOMIC_INDEPENDENT_HANDOFF_ACKNOWLEDGMENT_WITNESS_`, and `ROSIE_ECONOMIC_RETENTION_EXCEPTION_AGING_WITNESS_`. The issuer endpoints are respectively `/v1/economic-owner-decision-register-v770`, `/v1/economic-owner-handoff-ack-v770`, and `/v1/economic-retention-exception-aging-v770`.
- The website does not possess original private signing keys and never accepts arbitrary client provider URLs. The authenticated same-origin review endpoint enforces a 64 KB JSON body, no-redirect/no-store 16 KB external envelope cap, and rejects PUT/PATCH/DELETE with 405. Never put private keys, SQL, source exports or credentials into the browser. External original issuers must enforce their own replay prevention, signer custody, access policy, retention and write-once audit controls.
- No missing original issuer, source mismatch, signed reopen request or retention exception may be inferred away. The status `READY_FOR_SEPARATE_ORIGINAL_OWNER_DECISION_REVIEW` still requires a separately authenticated manual original-owner business decision; the website's business-mutation flags remain false even when software Production is GREEN.
