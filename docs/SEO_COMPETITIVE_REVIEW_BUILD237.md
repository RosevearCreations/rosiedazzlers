**Updated:** 2026-07-28

## Direction retained

- Keep one clear H1 per exposed page.
- Use compact, informative titles and headings that match what the page actually provides.
- Keep Oxford County, Norfolk County and town wording on genuinely relevant service/local pages rather than repeating locations unnaturally.
- Keep the Google Business Profile complete, accurate and consistent with the real business and website.
- Build prominence with approved real photos, reviews, useful local proof and links rather than ranking claims.

Rosie Dazzlers should keep its stronger operational/booking depth while replacing placeholders with approved local proof and avoiding duplicate thin pages.
