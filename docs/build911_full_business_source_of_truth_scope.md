# Rosie Dazzlers Build 911 — Full Business Source-of-Truth Scope Rebase

Owner scope broadened 19 November 2026. This is a **planning and original-source evidence inventory**, not an assertion that supplier, DAIP, bank, email, marketing, device, rental, storefront or remote-location operations are already live.

## What this build implements
- Signed-finance-user original Build898 source reconciliation is further summarized at `/api/admin/finance_acceptance_v911`: original Stripe/PayPal-related payment, webhook, refund and accounting/HST source counts/totals are visible with permanent independent-provider, bank, issued-invoice and accountant-signoff HOLD. No revenue or tax is invented from a zero-row source. No direct ledger/refund/tax mutation.
- Original DAIP media workflow index is linked and surfaced in the authenticated control desk. Real original projects/media/consent/processing remain separate proofs; zero original media records cannot certify DAIP.
- Staff-original supplier-review registry `public.rosie_supplier_reviews_v911` with service-role-only RLS, immutable private drafts, and `VEVOR.ca` supplier reference. Product name and exact supplier URL are entered only after verified by staff; acquisition can be `not_received/purchased/gifted/loaned/review_sample`. These states are **owner-reported**, never proof of physical custody, payment, sponsorship, product inventory receipt, cost basis, safety test or publisher authorization.
- Owner-initiated, URL-allowlisted **YouTube**, **Instagram**, **Facebook** embedded video preview cards in private review workbench (external platforms may set cookies). No autoplay or public publish. Gift/loan/sample automatically signals a material-connection disclosure requirement. DAIP project link must exist in original `creative_projects` and never implies permission to release media.
- Dark, responsive internal `/admin-finance-supplier-911.html` page with links from Admin/Finance/DAIP. Tests cover source-finance HOLD, provider missing evidence, DAIP consent, social URL trust boundaries and cross-site denial. No real device witnessed acceptance is falsely asserted.

## Canonical Rosie financial workflow
Original customer booking/quote, Stripe and PayPal provider callbacks, payment applications, refund request/reconciliation, bank import, accounting journal/issued invoice and tax review each keep their independent authority. The correct direct accounting integration is **validated provider success → idempotent booking/payment application → balanced journal/issued invoice → bank/fees/tax reconciliation → management analytics**. This build adds read-only acceptance visibility, not a second payment ledger. Actual automatic posting requires transactional uniqueness, reversal rules, original provider receipts and accountant-approved mapping before activation.

## Must-have business modules before considering 100%
1. **Customer experience**: desktop/tablet/mobile responsive login, account recovery, Smart and Manual booking, quote acceptance, product interest, real booking, service specials, private photos, communication and customer support.
2. **Commerce and physical operations**: complete product and service store, cart, shipping/tax/payment, pickup/drop-off/pop-up stand POS, merchandise returns and refunds, rental additions, event registration, safety waivers and location specials.
3. **Finance**: source-linked sales/refunds/payroll/HST, merchant settlements, exact amounts, idempotency, fees and reversals, balance sheet/GL, accountant evidence and current sales/revenue analytics.
4. **DAIP/social**: original camera intake → private R2 storage → cost/consent review → scene editing → media/story package → reviewed YouTube/Meta cards → owner-approved publication → attributable analytics. Material connections to supplier must be prominently disclosed. Product/brand tags require lawful permission and no invented endorsements.
5. **Security and privacy**: MFA for privileged roles, hardened role/capability scopes, RLS/keys/rotation, audit trail, CASL consent/withdrawal, PIPEDA/retention and deletion evidence, incident triage, backup/restore and access review.
6. **Company IT/support**: self-contained local health/traffic/performance/errors dashboards, downtime/reboot audit, helpdesk complaint cases/SLAs, site content editing, exception response, local support and status reporting, coordinated with three optional Master modules but not dependent on them for core business.
7. **Supplier review and stock**: VEVOR products for review as *actual items when entered*, acquisition and material-connection custody, costs, repair/reorder, equipment testing/safety, supplier/social video content and optional referral labels.
