# DAIP Content Generation and Marketing Automation

**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This module turns approved media into platform-ready marketing assets.

It should generate drafts, not publish automatically.

---

## 2. Required Package Per Detail

From one detailing job, prepare:

- 1 YouTube video
- 3 Facebook videos
- 5 Instagram Reels
- 5 TikTok videos
- before/after images
- website gallery
- Google Business Profile photos
- SEO metadata
- blog article
- thumbnail candidates
- platform captions

---

## 3. YouTube Video

Recommended output:

- 16:9 landscape
- 2–8 minutes
- full story arc
- branded intro/outro
- privacy-safe exports only
- title and description generated
- chapters if long enough

Suggested title pattern:

```text
Mobile Auto Detailing Transformation in [Town] | Rosie Dazzlers
```

---

## 4. Facebook Videos

Generate three versions:

1. Local trust video.
2. Transformation proof video.
3. Service-specific video.

Possible formats:

- 16:9 for page posts
- 4:5 for feed
- 1:1 for general reuse

---

## 5. Instagram Reels

Generate five vertical edits:

1. Hook transformation.
2. Satisfying cleaning moment.
3. Before/after reveal.
4. Service-specific clip.
5. Beauty/final reveal.

Recommended:

- 9:16
- 7–60 seconds
- caption-safe framing
- high-energy pacing

---

## 6. TikTok Videos

Generate five vertical edits with faster hooks:

1. Dirty-to-clean.
2. Foam/wash action.
3. Extraction/vacuum satisfying clip.
4. Final reveal.
5. Local service call-to-action.

---

## 7. Website Gallery

Generate gallery package:

- hero image
- before images
- after images
- process images
- service tags
- captions
- alt text
- town/service category
- structured metadata

---

## 8. Google Business Profile Photos

Generate/select:

- exterior after
- interior after
- technician at work
- before/after proof
- local service area relevance
- mobile setup/van if available

Captions should be simple and local.

---

## 9. SEO Package

Generate:

- SEO title
- meta description
- image alt text
- internal link suggestions
- service keywords
- town keywords
- schema notes
- blog slug

Example keywords:

- mobile auto detailing Tillsonburg
- car interior shampoo Oxford County
- pet hair removal car detailing
- ceramic spray protection
- headlight restoration

---

## 10. Blog Article

Blog should include:

- town/service context
- vehicle type
- problem areas
- services performed
- process summary
- before/after outcome
- maintenance tips
- call to action

No private customer data should be included.

---

## 11. Captions

Generate platform-specific captions.

Facebook: local trust and service detail.  
Instagram: concise, visual, hashtag-friendly.  
TikTok: punchy, hook-first.  
YouTube: descriptive and SEO-friendly.  
GBP: simple, local, service-oriented.

---

## 12. Review Before Publish

Every generated marketing item must go through review:

- approve
- edit
- reject
- regenerate
- schedule
- publish/export

---

## 13. Attribution Tracking

Each published asset should link back to:

- media_job_id
- export_id
- service type
- town
- platform
- campaign

This allows future analytics and ROI tracking.
