ⓘ Contextual help and safeguards

Does the owner decision register approve a closure?

No. It authenticates a separate original-issuer signed register entry bound to earlier original owner decisions and independent receipts. Review readiness and an external register claim cannot record actual business acceptance in this application.

What does the handoff acknowledgment mean?

A separate witness must sign the exact original delivery-manifest digest and recipient acknowledgment, after the register entry. A disputed or missing acknowledgment remains HOLD and must be reconciled externally.

How do we calculate exception aging?

From the original issuer-signed snapshot time and oldest open exception timestamp. Open or critical exceptions, an exceeded source threshold, disputed custody, or expired signed retention period all remain HOLD; no case closes automatically.
