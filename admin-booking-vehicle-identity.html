Vehicle identity operating help

  1. Use the booking’s existing customer profile as the ownership boundary.
  2. Choose only the saved vehicle that actually received the service. Never guess from similar vehicle names.
  3. If a booking has no customer profile, resolve that customer identity through the normal customer workflow first.
  4. Unlink only when the previous relationship is known to be wrong.
  5. This workbench does not alter service completion, appointment capacity, quotes, charges, memberships or recurring schedules.
