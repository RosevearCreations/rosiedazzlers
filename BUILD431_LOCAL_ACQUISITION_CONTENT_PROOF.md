# Build 431 — Local Acquisition & Content Proof

## Purpose

Use first-party traffic, genuine local proof, service-area evidence and dated provider evidence to improve local pages and content priorities without inferring Google ranking success.

## Evidence model

Build 431 may combine bounded evidence from:

- first-party landing-page and booking-funnel traffic;
- genuine before/after and service proof already approved for public use;
- local-search provider evidence already saved through existing authority;
- town/service-page coverage;
- service education pages and customer journey evidence;
- stale, missing or unavailable provider evidence.

## Content workflow

The preferred operator view identifies:

- local/service pages with weak first-party engagement;
- pages missing current genuine proof;
- duplicate or thin service explanations;
- gaps between public copy and actual services offered;
- content candidates worth improving next.

Provider evidence must remain dated and attributed. No search rank, GBP performance or Search Console outcome may be inferred when provider evidence is absent.

## SEO boundary

Maintain one meaningful H1 per indexable page, truthful service-area claims, canonical/metadata integrity and no fabricated reviews, locations or business claims.

## Mutation boundary

No automatic publishing to third-party providers, no fake review generation, no fabricated location content, no DNS change, no schema migration unless separately approved, no customer outreach, no ad-spend mutation and no permanent polling.

## Acceptance

The exact candidate must pass focused Local Acquisition & Content Proof authority, retained SEO/local-acquisition/media authorities, Current Source Gate, exact Development acceptance, protected-main PR checks and exact Production deployment/runtime/business acceptance.


## Implemented source authority

The staff-only `/admin-seo-tasks.html` surface now includes a Build 431 workbench backed by `/api/admin/local_acquisition_content_proof`. It combines bounded 30-day page-path rollups, genuine published public-approved non-sample before/after proof, existing local SEO task cards and the retained Build 414 Search Console / Google Business Profile evidence classifications.

Content candidates are prioritized for operator review when first-party engagement is weak or absent in the observed window, genuine current proof is missing, or existing local SEO work is still open. The browser also performs same-origin deployed-page checks for H1 count, canonical and description presence, copy depth, duplicate-copy review and service-topic review. Those checks are heuristics for human review only and never become ranking/indexing/Maps claims.

Missing analytics, proof, task or provider evidence remains `unavailable`, `provider_dependent` or `owner_action` as appropriate. Build 431 remains read-only and schema-neutral.
