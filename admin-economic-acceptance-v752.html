What is actually approved here?

Nothing. This workbench produces evidence-linked candidates and unsigned local notes; separate recorded owner authorization is required before any pricing edit or economic acceptance. Source corrections must be verified at the owning booking, inventory, staff-time or finance workflow.

Why does a clean comparison still need acceptance?
