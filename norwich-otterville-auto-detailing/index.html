Rural dust and work use can move a job beyond maintenance

Norwich and Otterville vehicles can collect gravel-road dust, work-site soil, winter salt, pet hair and family-use contamination in combinations that are hard to price from vehicle size alone. A routine wash or interior refresh is different from embedded carpet contamination, repeated extraction, heavy salt, odor-source work, paint correction or specialist restoration.

Photos help us distinguish a maintenance problem from a restoration-level job before arrival. Any deeper labour is confirmed rather than silently added.

What to prepare

  • A legal, stable work location with enough safe clearance around the vehicle.
  • Keys/access and belongings removed from areas being cleaned.
  • Known leaks, electrical concerns, recent repairs, coatings, tint or fragile materials disclosed.
  • Rosie Dazzlers supplies normal operating water and power; customer utilities are not the standard service requirement.
