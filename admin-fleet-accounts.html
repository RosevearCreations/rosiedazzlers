Fleet pipeline operating help

  1. New means the public assessment request needs staff review.
  2. Use Reviewing while checking vehicle count, condition, service area, site access and media.
  3. Use Contacted after an actual customer follow-up attempt.
  4. Create / open draft quote reuses an existing quote linked to this fleet lead or creates one draft with zero quoted/accepted dollars. It does not create a customer account or booking.
  5. Complete pricing and scope in the Quote dashboard. Use Quoted here only after the quote has actually been prepared/sent; draft handoff does not change the fleet status automatically.
  6. Converted records are read-only here. Conversion evidence must still come from an approved booking/quote workflow.
  7. Repeat-service interest is only a preference. Do not promise automatic scheduling, a discount, recurring billing or guaranteed capacity.
