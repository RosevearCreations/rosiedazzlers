ⓘ Help and safeguards

Does a signed handoff decision register execute closure?

No. No. The source signs an independent register claim. The real owner and custodian must separately execute any business closure.

When is dispute aging escalation required?

The independent source signs elapsed hours, open disputes, the prior aging register and its source-derived alert threshold. Any unresolved, conflicting or overdue escalation remains HOLD.

Does a continuity witness prove original retention enforcement?

No. It is an authenticated original-source history and retention-continuity claim with a bounded expiry. Actual storage enforcement and closure require independent original custodian and owner action.
