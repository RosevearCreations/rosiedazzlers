ⓘ What does this review prove?

Why doesn't a matching Supabase row close the case?

The row proves current identity and booking association only. The original correction actor, earlier revision and immutable custody chain require independent source history not provided by a current-row lookup.

What is owner-decision persistence readiness?

A non-mutating probe for a dedicated economic_owner_closure_decision_audit table. Even if visible, the tool will not infer append-only restrictions, signing authority or the ability to write. This build never creates tables or submits owner decisions.

Can we override HOLD on this screen?

No. We must independently authenticate the original source history and separately authorize a properly audited closure workflow. All price, travel, inventory, payroll, customer, booking, seasonal and provider settings remain unchanged.
