ⓘ Help and safeguards

Does a signed resolution receipt close an existing dispute?

No. It authenticates an independent issuer's statement about the exact original dispute register. The real custodian and original owner must separately verify any change.

What does sign-off continuity mean?

A new original-owner signed manual request is compared with the independent historical register and witnessed by a separate issuer. The site cannot sign off for the owner or alter production records.

How is retention SLA aging reviewed?

The external witness signs age, critical count, policy digest and result. The original signed aging threshold limits the claim; an overdue or conflicting result stays HOLD for external escalation.
